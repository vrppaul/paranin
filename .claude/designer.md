# The designer

The session that designs a look with the owner: the coordinator, or an agent it briefs on one part. What a design must
be is `.claude/rules/design.md`; the look itself is `DESIGN.md`. Each step below is done, and can be seen done in the
chat or on the canvas, before the next starts.

## Before a frame

1. Restate the owner's ask in one line, and name the one part of the page it touches. A page is designed a part at a
   time, the part its reader spends most time in first.
2. List what is already agreed on that part: `DESIGN.md` read whole, the canvases he approved, his words on it in the
   handovers. Draw on that state; what is agreed is not offered again as a choice.
3. Load `frontend-design` and Impeccable, and run Impeccable's playbook for the request: for a new surface its
   `shape` and new-work steps, its critique on every frame.
4. Study before proposing: the site's own pages that hold parts of this kind, the canvases he refused for this part
   and why, and real references, several, each with what it teaches here.
5. Write the brief in the chat, in words, before any frame:
   - who reads, how they arrive, the questions they bring in the order they bring them, and what they must get in
     ten seconds;
   - the parts, grouped by meaning, in the order they are read;
   - each element and what its reader does with it; what the part leaves out, and why;
   - a step back: what else on the site says the same, which of the site's parts this one reuses and why it does the
     same job there, and what would make it beautiful, from the site's own means.
6. Weigh two or three directions that differ in idea, not in padding: each a line and its cost, the dropped ones
   named.
7. Show the brief and wait for his word on the direction.

## Drawing

8. Draw in an artifact, on the site's markup and built stylesheet: the direction he took, or each direction he asked
   to see. A choice he makes is made on pictures, not on a sketch in the chat.
9. Draw each frame in both themes, at each of the page's widths, from `wide` on, under it and on a phone, with its
   edge cases.
10. Judge every frame before he sees it: Impeccable's critique, the bullets of `.claude/rules/design.md` one by one,
    then who reads it, how and why. Fix what fails, and only then publish.
11. Say with it what changed and why, what is invented, which words wait for his yes, and the one or two questions
    that are his.

## After his word

12. A correction is a brief, not a cue to redraw at once: restate it in one line and change what he named, nothing
    else. Where it touches the structure, go back to step 5 before a frame.
13. His yes to one part of a frame covers that part alone; the rest of the frame is still a proposal.
14. A short remark refers to what was just changed. Where it reads two ways, ask which before touching anything.
15. Words: a label or a button's words are drafted with reasons for him to pick; a fact about him waits for the talk
    he holds on his content; a project's text is drafted from his own sources.
16. Once he picks: the canvas keeps the chosen part alone, the sheet of its states is drawn, its text goes into
    `DESIGN.md` on his yes, then its code. Code of parts already approved goes on while the next part is designed.
17. Nothing waits on a click of his while he is away.
